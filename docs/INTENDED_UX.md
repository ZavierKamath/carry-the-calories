# INTENDED UX

The general UX theme is that each daily note logs calories in what appears to be a sheet of paper. 

Log names are written plain, simple, and concise on the left with calories and protein on the right in a different color.

Elements are added up and totaled at the bottom (if overflow, total appears overlayed slightly and minimally at bottom) almost appearing as if it's an elementary school addition assignment but with a more polished and minimal design.

While AI is processing the dictation (happens in real time, doesn't wait for dictation to end) it builds up buffers of audio and text to create entries that are named minimally and intuitively.

During processing, animations show where AI is doing work in a minimal and smooth and satisfying way. AI names and comes up with calories and protein.

Past days can be seen and added to but that is a separate 'past' tab. 
Along with past days, simple plots of past timeframes and averages can also be seen.

Manual adding (no dictation) of logs is possible and easy, but isn't the primary user flow
